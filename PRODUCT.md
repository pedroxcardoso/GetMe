# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary user is the owner of this personal desktop app, using Windows and Brazilian Portuguese.

## Product Purpose

GETME turns a supported media link into a locally saved file. Success means the selected audio or video is saved to the user's chosen folder and remains easy to find in the local history.

## Positioning

GETME runs on the user's computer: media downloads go directly from the platform to the selected local folder without an app-operated relay or account.

## Operating Context

The user pastes one link, chooses an available format and quality, chooses a destination, and follows progress in the app. The app includes local settings and a searchable download history.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- YouTube supports MP4 video or MP3 audio and a maximum resolution choice.
- X, Instagram Reels, and TikTok support MP4 video and a maximum resolution choice.
- yt-dlp and FFmpeg are required for supported downloads and conversions.
- Settings and successful download history are stored locally; the destination defaults to Downloads.
- The app does not use browser cookies or sign-in credentials and does not bypass private-content or login restrictions.
- Users must save only media they own or are authorized to keep.
- The existing React and TypeScript renderer is packaged in Electron. The current user-facing installer is for Windows.

## Product Principles

- Keep downloads local and explain when a required tool is missing.
- Make platform, format, quality, destination, and progress clear before and during a download.
- Preserve user settings and successful-download history on the device.
- Honor platform restrictions and the user's authorization to save the media.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Keep text and controls legible against dark surfaces and preserve visible keyboard focus.
- Keep the saved light/dark appearance toggle available.
- Use Brazilian Portuguese and pair status colors with readable labels or icons.
